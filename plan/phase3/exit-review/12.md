an `RM-` label on an `MR-` rule's carriers — `RM-2`, `RM-3` and `RM-15`, which spec 12
does not amend, and `RM-16`, whose carriers were MR-16's burst refusals rather than
RM-16's stop — and eleven amended rules had no row: the six Kernel rules KB-1 and
KB-2 amend, `MR-1`, `MR-4`, `MR-14`, `MR-17` and RM-16 itself. The four rows are

`MA-6`'s handle list (`InputStore` is in no role signature), and no Plugin host
exists for `MA-46`.

VB-10's carriers and ceilings in the `RM-16`, `MR-18`, `MR-25` and `MR-30` rows.
