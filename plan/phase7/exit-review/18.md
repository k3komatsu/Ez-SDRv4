# `18` — the UHD Radio Module disposition table

Every rule of spec 18 ([`design/18-uhd-radio.md`](../../../design/18-uhd-radio.md)), in its order. `read from` gives each test source and function so the assertion can be inspected directly. Unless a cell names another file, the tests are in `crates/ezsdr-radio-uhd/tests/fake.rs` (`FakeDevice`, through the real coordinator unless the test is a unit test), with the shared fixtures and the rehearsal bodies of B3–B7 in `tests/common/mod.rs`; `uhd_api.rs` and `hardware.rs` are the crate's other two test files (feature `uhd`). Bench records are cited by their section of [`bench-results.md`](../bench-results.md): "Session 1" (B0–B5), "Session 2" (B6, B7, B8, the B5 rerun), and "Session 2, part N" (N = 2…11). Mutation ids (`U…`, `R…`, `B…`, `G…`) are rows of `plan/phase7/tools/mutations.json`, each killed by the test named with it (spec 18 §8, GZ-6). A clause that no test body asserts is written out in its row as **Not asserted** and listed in the [README](README.md).
