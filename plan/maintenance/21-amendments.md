## VG-1 — A stream's end moves only earlier: a later stop never extends a receive stream (#46, its first case)

**Problem.** [#46](https://github.com/k3komatsu/Ez-SDRv4/issues/46), case (1): a MockRadio receive stream ended by a `Stop` of `<id>/rx` restarts on a later `Provider::stop(Orderly)`. Each claim, re-checked:

- VERIFIED — the issue's case (1) reproduces (probe, 1 MS/s, a `Stop` of `mock/rx` at T0 + 1 ms, `stop(Orderly)` at T0 + 5 ms): on `ideal` the blocks after the stop are `[1 000, 3 000)` and `[3 000, 5 000)`, on `x310-like` `[2 000, 4 000)` and `[4 000, 6 000)`, none flagged. The cause is as the issue says: `stop_rx` overwrites `rx.end` without reading the end already set (`crates/ezsdr-mock-radio/src/lib.rs:877-882`), while `rx.next` stands at the `Stop`'s end. The overwrite is in `6bf67fb`, the commit that introduced `stop_rx` (VERIFIED: `git show`).
- VERIFIED, beyond the issue — a second `Stop` of `mock/rx`, or a `Stop` of `mock`, at T0 + 5 ms extends the stream the same way (blocks `[1 000, 3 000)`, `[3 000, 5 000)`, `[5 000, 5 001)` on `ideal`), since both reach `stop_rx` (`lib.rs:897-906`; the device `Stop` runs `stop`, `:899`). `stop(Abort)` after the `Stop` delivers nothing more (`rx.end = rx.next`), so today an abort moves the end earlier and an orderly stop or a `Stop` moves it later.
- VERIFIED, beyond the issue (the issue left it INFERRED) — in a Run, the extension reaches the recorder when the `Stop` falls within about a block of the Run's orderly end on a profile with a stop tail. A Spec Run on `x310-like` capturing from T0, its schedule a `Stop` of `radio/rx` at T0 + 4 ms (the stream's end at sample 5 000) and `Stop {}` at T0 + 5 ms: the artifact holds `[0, 6 000)` with no gap — 1 000 samples after the stream's end — and the cleanup's drain runs to T0 + 5.999 001 ms (probe; with the `Stop` at T0 + 4.5 ms, 6 000 samples for an end at 5 500). With the `Stop` at T0 + 1 ms, or on `ideal`, nothing reaches the recorder: the extension's next block ends before the stop instant, MockRadio schedules no wakeup in the past (`design/09-mock-radio.md:191`), and RS-6 step 3's drain, which steps only at wakeups, stops at once — which is why the issue's Session closed by `finish` showed nothing.
- VERIFIED, beyond the issue — the UHD Module keeps the earlier cut when an orderly `Provider::stop` or a second `Stop` follows a `Stop` (`crates/ezsdr-radio-uhd/src/provider/rx.rs:275-286`, `stop_at`'s early return and minimum; FakeDevice probe: no block after the `Stop`'s cut, one `rx_stop`), and a `cold` switch keeps an earlier cut (`rx.rs:198-207`). But `Provider::stop(Abort)` replaces the cut whatever it was (`rx.rs:215-226`): uhd-rx alone, a `Stop`'s cut at sample 1 000 and then an abort's at 5 ms leave the cut at 5 ms, and the samples that arrive next are published up to sample 5 000 (unit probe); the same assignment replaces a switch's earlier cut at `e₁` (unit probe: a switch to 0 channels with `e₁` at sample 1 000, then the abort, publishes up to 5 000), past the instant at which uhd-control has already ended the old clock (UR-25). Through the threads this needs samples past the `Stop`'s cut to reach uhd-rx between the abort's `Cut` and its `Shutdown` (`crates/ezsdr-radio-uhd/src/provider/mod.rs:483-490`) — a receive latency longer than the joins between them, which a transmit burst still open stretches to 100 ms, uhd-tx waiting for its last `BURST_ACK` (UR-26) (INFERRED: not observed end to end).
- VERIFIED — no rule says what a later stop does to a receive stream that has ended. RM-16's receive half "keeps delivering until stop instant + `radio.timing.stop_tail_ns` and then ends" (`design/07-radio-model.md:147`); MR-25 (`design/09-mock-radio.md:143`) and UR-26 (`design/18-uhd-radio.md:179`) say the same. The transmit half has its rule since Phase 3: "a burst that had already ended keeps its end" (RM-16, VB-1).
- INFERRED (the issue's) — results of experiments that use `Stop` may differ between MockRadio and the UHD Module, which Phase 8's parity would meet. Agreed for this case, the UHD Module keeping the earlier cut on an orderly stop. No claim of the issue is wrong.

**Decision for the owner.** What a later `Stop` or `Provider::stop` does to a receive stream whose end is already set.

**Recommendation.** An end moves only earlier: a receive stream whose end is already set — by a `Stop`, by `Provider::stop`, by a `cold` change's `e₁` or by a loss — keeps the earlier of that end and the new one. A later orderly stop or `Stop` never extends a stream; an abort, or a loss (MR-20), still ends one in its tail at once. This is the transmit half's existing rule ("a burst that had already ended keeps its end"), stated for the receive half, in the same words for both Providers.

*Under the pre-release premise:* nothing changed; no alternative was weighed on cost.

`design/07-radio-model.md` RM-16, after "under `abort` it ends at once." (sentence added):

> A receive stream whose end is already set — by a `Stop` for `<device>/rx` or `<device>`, by `Provider::stop`, by a `cold` change's `e₁` (RM-25) or by a loss — keeps the earlier of that end and the one a later stop gives it, whether that end has passed or its tail still runs: an end moves only earlier, so a later orderly stop or `Stop` never extends a stream, and an `abort` still ends one in its tail at once (spec 21, VG-1; issue #46).

The three texts name the same ends in each Provider's terms: a `Stop` on both; a `cold` change's `e₁`, which MockRadio sets when the change takes effect and the UHD Module when uhd-control books it (UR-25); and MockRadio's simulated loss (MR-20). **Rejected alternatives.**
- *The later stop's end wins (today's MockRadio).* An orderly stop then delivers, unflagged, samples after the stream's end — in a Run, into an artifact (above).
- *The first end set wins, whatever comes later.* An abort could no longer cut an orderly tail short, against RM-16's "under `abort` it ends at once" and MA-13's immediate abort; and a loss inside a tail could not end the stream at its instant (MR-20, VF-3).
- *Once a `Stop` has run, `Provider::stop` leaves the receive side alone.* The same objection: the abort's immediacy is lost.

## VG-2 — A receive stream a `Stop` has ended stays stopped until `radio.start_rx`; a transmit `Stop` ends bursts, not the stream (#46, its second case)

**Problem.** [#46](https://github.com/k3komatsu/Ez-SDRv4/issues/46), case (2): a later `cold` change restarts a MockRadio receive stream a `Stop` of `<id>/rx` has ended. Each claim, re-checked:

- VERIFIED — the issue's case (2) reproduces (probe, a `Stop` of `mock/rx` at T0 + 1 ms, a `cold` `radio.rx.sample_rate_hz` = 2 MS/s at T0 + 3 ms): on `ideal` a new receive clock starts at T0 + 3 ms and its blocks run to sample 394 000 by T0 + 200 ms; on `x310-like` the new clock starts at T0 + 53 ms, after the restart lead. A `cold` change of `radio.rx.channels` from 1 to 2 does the same. The cause is as the issue says: the receive `cold` path (`lib.rs:1229-1257`) ends the old clock at `e₁` when it has not ended and registers a new clock with a new `Rx` (`end: None`, `:1252`) whenever the new count is above 0 and `rx` has a link; it never reads `rx.end`. The issue's Python reproduction stands at `3ccd68d` (no code changed since `642b8bd`).
- VERIFIED, beyond the issue — a `cold` change received before the `Stop` and effective after it restarts the stream too: received at T0 + 0.5 ms for T0 + 3 ms, a new clock at T0 + 3 ms (`ideal`) or T0 + 53 ms (`x310-like`); for T0 + 1.5 ms, inside `x310-like`'s 1 ms tail, the change first cuts the tail at `e₁` (the last old block `[0, 1 500)`) and starts a new clock at T0 + 51.5 ms.
- VERIFIED, beyond the issue (the issue left it INFERRED) — the UHD Module restarts a stopped receive stream the same way. uhd-control books a `Stop` of `<id>/rx` by handing uhd-rx a `RxCmd::Stop` (`crates/ezsdr-radio-uhd/src/provider/control.rs:189-201`) and never updates its own view of the stream (`streams.rx`, set at `start`, `mod.rs:448`), so a later `cold` change books a switch (`control.rs:412-481`): it ends the old clock at `e₁`, registers a new one at `e₂` and hands uhd-rx a `Switch`, which uhd-rx, its stream ended at the `Stop`'s cut, carries out at once (`rx.rs:163-169, 500-525`) — it reopens the streamer, configures the direction and issues `rx_start(e₂)`. *How a start and a `Stop` reach a Provider.* VERIFIED, by reading and, for the last point, by a probe:
- A `Vocabulary` verb declared `CompileRule::PeripheralCommand {}` compiles to `Action::PeripheralCommand` addressed to the resource the verb names, with RS-19's `at` (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/session.rs:586-593`; pinned by `rs_19_vocabulary_action_carries_a_time`, `crates/ezsdr-kernel/tests/run_session.rs:1394-1403`). An explicit `at` is passed through, one already past included. Admission checks no deadline of a `PeripheralCommand`: KC-24 has no step for it beyond the target and its params' values (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/coordinator/admission.rs:49-160`), and KC-19's lead check reads only `RejectAtPlan` bursts (`pipeline.rs:1100-1145`). RS-49 restricts its target to no kind.
- A `Stop` carries no time: a Provider stops at the instant it handles it (MR-25; UR-26).
- A Spec Run's timed templates, `PeripheralCommand` among them, are admitted at `arm` and dispatched at the start round, ahead of their instants, while an untimed template (`Stop`) waits in the coordinator's agenda until its instant (KC-17, KC-18: `design/06-kernel-coordinator.md:432-433`), and the coordinator dispatches the agenda items due at a wakeup before that wakeup's stepping round (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/coordinator/stepping.rs:419-420`). A Session's entries are dispatched each in a round of its own at the current instant (KC-21, KC-28), and on a device-paced Session the coordinator waits until the Provider has finished with each before the next (KC-21a).
- MockRadio handles the Actions it receives in a step before the events due at that step's instant (§4), so the order of a `Stop` and a pending command due at the same instant depends on whether the `Stop` comes in the round of that instant or in a later one: the review's probe, rerun on today's tree — a receive `cold` rate change due at T0 + 1 ms, queued the way draft 3 queued a start, and a `Stop` of `mock/rx` at T0 + 1 ms — gives 199 (`ideal`) and 149 (`x310-like`) blocks on the new clock by T0 + 200 ms when the `Stop` comes in the change's own round, and none when it comes in a later round at the same instant.

So a start judged when it arrives would make a Spec's "`Stop` at 1 s, start at 2 s" do nothing (the start arrives at the start round), and a fixed "`Stop` first" at one instant is not a property either Provider has.

**Decision for the owner.** What an update does to a receive stream that a `Stop` has ended, or whose `Stop` tail is running; and how a client receives again. The options weighed: (a) apply the value and keep the stream stopped, the way back being a change of the channel count to 0 and back (draft 1); (b) refuse the update; (c) restart the stream — today's behaviour in both Providers, and UC-3's literal reading — written into the rules; (d) apply the value and keep the stream stopped until an explicit start. Within (d): the start's form; when a start takes effect against `Stop`s and `cold` changes, at one instant and on hardware; how far back its `e₁` may reach; its lead; and what a `Stop` for `<device>` cancels.

**Recommendation.** (d), with the start as the verb `radio.start_rx`. A receive stream a `Stop` has ended is *stopped*: an update of its configuration applies its value and starts nothing — a `cold` one is carried out as a change to 0 channels is, the stream ending at `e₁` if its tail still runs then and its clock at `e₁` if it has not ended. The stream starts again on `radio.start_rx`, a `Session` verb compiled to a `PeripheralCommand` on `<radio>/rx` with no params — in Python `Rx.start(at=None)`. The start is UC-3's restart with the stopped stream as the old one, and its rule is one for both Providers:

- **States.** A receive stream *runs*, is *stopped*, or does not exist (its count is 0). A `Stop` stops a running stream (VG-1 keeps its end); a start on a stopped stream makes it run on a new clock, so a later `Stop` stops it again and a later `cold` change restarts it as UC-3 says; a `cold` change to a stopped stream leaves it stopped; a change to 0 removes it and one from 0 starts it (RM-21). A start on a running stream, or with no channel or no link, changes nothing, and so does a `Stop` while the count is 0.
- **When a start takes effect.** At `e`, its `at` or the instant the radio receives it if that is later, never before T0, and it is decided then, not when it arrives ahead of `e`: on a stopped stream with a receive channel and a link it takes effect, and the stream runs from `e`; on any other it changes nothing. Starts, `Stop`s and `cold` changes of a stream are taken — a start decided at `e`, a `Stop` handled, a `cold` change received — in the order of those instants and, at one instant, in the order the radio received them: a start received before a `Stop` whose instant is at or after the start's `e` is decided before that `Stop`. That order decides which changes a start waits for; each change still takes effect at its own instants (UC-2, RM-25). `Rx.start()` without `at` sends the Session's current instant, so it takes effect when the radio receives it, and Session calls act in the order made. A start has no lateness: an `at` already past means "on arrival", with no event.
- **What it does.** Its new clock waits for every `cold` change of the stream received before the start was decided: `e₁` is the latest of `e`, the origin of the stream's clock, the instant that clock ended if it has, those changes' `e₁`s and the Provider's own floor, on the clock's lattice while it runs, and the clock ends there if it has not ended. The UHD Module's floor is the instant it decides the start, within its 1 ms poll, and the instant the stream a `Stop` met has surely ended: the `Stop`'s instant plus the receive call then in progress and the delivery allowance (UR-25's term); MockRadio has none. The stream ends at `e₁` if its tail still runs; the new clock starts at `e₂`, the first instant at or after `e₁` plus the restart lead on its own lattice, with the configuration in effect at `e₂`, its first block carrying tick 0. Until `e₂` the stream runs with no sample: a `Stop` stops it again, and a `cold` change received after the start was decided restarts it as UC-3 says.
- **Never before an origin.** No `e₁` — a start's, or a receive `cold` change's — is earlier than the origin of the clock it ends: a clock a `Stop` met before its first sample ends at its origin, with no sample on it, when the next start or change comes.
- **On hardware (guidance, not a rule).** A `Stop`'s instant is when the UHD Module handles it, some time after the coordinator's dispatch — by reading, the Provider's Action queue and uhd-control's 1 ms poll, a few milliseconds on an idle host, and a loaded host can stretch it (INFERRED; not measured: *Follow-up issues*, 8). A start that a Spec or a Reactor times too close after an untimed `Stop` may then take effect before the `Stop` on the UHD Module, which leaves the stream stopped, and after it on MockRadio, which restarts it. Such a start is best scheduled at least the radio's restart lead (`radio.timing.restart_lead_ns`, 50 ms on `x310-like` and the UHD profiles) after the `Stop`. A Session is unaffected: on a Simulation Session each submission gets a round of its own (KC-21), and on a device-paced one each call returns only once the Provider has finished with the Action — for the UHD Module, once uhd-control has booked it (KC-21a; `crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/coordinator/paced.rs:206-239`, `state.rs:84-95`). A start at a `Stop`'s own instant in a Spec is received first, at the start round (KC-17), so it comes first and the stream stays stopped: a Spec schedules the start after the `Stop`.

A change of the count to 0 and from 0 still starts a stream, as RM-21 says. A `Stop` for `<device>` cancels the pending `hardware_timed` updates only: a `cold` change or a start booked before it takes effect at its instant, so the stream started again runs at the configuration the Kernel holds, and the faults stay armed. The transmit side keeps its rule, written down: a `Stop` ends its bursts, not its stream, and its next burst is its start. Both Providers.

*The stop/resume story.* A user calls `sdr.rx.stop()`: reception ends, after the radio's stop tail, and stays ended. Between that and `sdr.rx.start()` they may retune, change the rate or the channel count: each change is admitted, applied to the radio and read back (`sdr.rx.sample_rate`, KC-27), and none resumes reception; a `capture` meanwhile raises `CaptureTimeout`, since nothing arrives. `sdr.rx.start()` resumes when the radio receives it — the new SampleClock begins the restart lead later, with the configuration then in force and its first sample at tick 0 — so the next `capture` returns fresh samples with exact timestamps; `sdr.rx.start(at=sdr.after(0.5))` resumes then instead. Calls act in the order made: `stop(); start()` restarts, `start(); stop()` leaves the stream stopped, and a second `start` while it runs changes nothing. On the transmit side `sdr.tx.stop()` ends the waveform and the next `repeat` starts again. The same holds on MockRadio and on the UHD Module, and in a Spec Run, whose schedule says "stop at 1 s, start at 2 s". The Manifest shows a new receive clock per start that takes effect — and a clock ended at its own origin, carrying no sample, where a `Stop` met a stream before its first sample and a start followed (and, on the UHD Module, where a `Stop` cancelled a switch booked before it: *Follow-up issues*, 5).

Why (d), for the long term:
- *A lifecycle Action is undone only by an Action that says so.* `Stop` is a Kernel lifecycle verb (RS-50); a `cold` change is a configuration change. Under (c) a configuration change resumes what the user stopped, and a `hardware_timed` change of the same stream does not, so whether reception resumes depends on the key's update class, which the user does not see.
- *One way back, the same on every Provider, at once.* (a)'s way back — the count to 0, then back — is refused by the UHD Module until the old stream's `e₁` (UR-25; Review M, N-4), and MockRadio accepts it at once: the review's probe, reproduced, shows both changes admitted, one `rx_start`, the configuration holding one channel and the rejection "UR-25: the stream changed to 0 channels is still draining; enable it after its e₁". A recipe whose second step must wait for an instant the user cannot see, and fails silently on one Provider, is not a way back. `start_rx` is honoured on both, at the instant it names.
- *Symmetry.* Each direction has a stop and an explicit start, and no configuration change resumes either: for transmit, the next burst (VERIFIED above); for receive, `start_rx`.
- *v3 had this pair.* v3's continuous receive kept a flag, `_isStreaming`, set at construction (`v3/source/controller/cyclicrx.d:26-31`) and changed only by the client's "loop receive start" and "loop receive end" commands (`:280-298`), and its pause and resume — the practice Vision §27 names as the precursor of `cold` (`design/vision/05-coherence-calibration-and-runtime-semantics.md:153`) — stopped and restarted reception only `if(_isStreaming)` (`:67-75, 149-162`). A stop the client made survived reconfiguration, and only the client's start resumed it (VERIFIED, by reading). v3's client exposed the pair as `startReceiveLoop` and `stopReceiveLoop` (`v3/client/ezsdr.py:238-246`) and used it, with a timed start, around setting the device's time (`v3/client/ezsdr.py:306-324, 381-398`; `v3/client/examples/test_v3_TXRX.py:29-36`, `test_continuous_recv.py:35-44`), a use v4's T0 replaces (§61's behaviours 3 and 4); the meaning of the pair is the behaviour kept (§61: behaviour compatibility, not wire compatibility).
- *The configuration stays truthful.* KC-27 records what the Kernel admitted, and a Provider that refuses at run time leaves the refused value there (`design/06-kernel-coordinator.md:479`). Under (d) the Provider takes every value a stopped stream is given, and a device `Stop` no longer drops a booked `cold` change on MockRadio, so the stream started again runs at what `sdr.rx.sample_rate` reads back, on both Providers.

Why the verb, and why this timing rule:
- *A start is a command, not configuration.* As a verb it leaves KC-27's configuration alone — draft 2's trigger key stayed there as `radio.rx.start: True` while the stream was stopped again, entered every later KA-6 coerce, needed an RM-4 row, a `coerce` exception and a rule for a Spec that constrains it, and, as a `cold` update, fell under every `cold` rule written for configuration. None of that remains.
- *It has a time, like its siblings.* `start_repeat` and `send` are verbs on `<radio>/tx` with RS-19's `at`; `start_rx` is the same form on `<radio>/rx`, the very resource `rx.stop()` names, and a Session can time it (`SetParameter` carries no `at`, RS-14).
- *It needs no Kernel change.* A `PeripheralCommand` verb compiles to the named target already, and nothing in the Kernel judges its `at` (above). The Vision's examples of the Action are a smart antenna's command and a peripheral reaching a radio's GPIO bank, a sub-resource of the radio served by the radio's own Provider (`design/vision/01-purpose-and-core-boundary.md:324-338`; §39, `design/vision/07-peripherals-and-host-io.md:62-84`): a Vocabulary command to a resource of a Provider instance, which is what `start_rx` is. Its name is the only friction (*Follow-up issues*, 7).
- *Decided at its instant.* A start is judged when it takes effect, not when it arrives, because a Spec Run hands it to the Provider at the start round (KC-17, KC-18) and a Reactor may emit it ahead of its instant: judged on arrival, "stop at 1 s, start at 2 s" would do nothing.
- *At one instant, by arrival.* A `Stop` has no instant but its handling, so arrival is the one order both Providers can keep whatever the round: MockRadio by having every stop first decide the pending starts due at or before it, uhd-control by deciding the held starts whose `e` has come before it books any Action. Draft 3's "`Stop` first" was MockRadio's behaviour only when the `Stop` came in the start's own round — a Spec's agenda, not a Session (above) — and the UHD Module did the opposite for the same Spec.
- *Running from `e`, not from the first sample.* A start whose clock must wait for a `cold` change could instead be decided only when the change is in effect. A `cold` change takes effect some 55 ms after its booking on the UHD Module and at once on MockRadio (UR-25's floor; *Follow-up issues*, 1), so `rx.stop(); rx.sample_rate = …; rx.start(); rx.stop()` would end running on the UHD Module, the start decided after the second `stop()`, and stopped on MockRadio (INFERRED, by reading: the alternative was not prototyped). Decided at `e`, the stream runs from the start's instant on both Providers, a `stop()` after it stops it, and only the instant of its first sample depends on the floor.
- *An untimed start acts at once.* RS-19 gives an untimed `PeripheralCommand` the target's command lead, which put a start after a `Stop` submitted right after it; `Rx.start()` sends the current instant instead. On a device-paced Session that instant has passed when the radio receives the start, which means "on arrival": VERIFIED end to end on a FakeDevice Session, no late event. The Python test checks the Simulation Session.
- *Its clock after the changes before it.* The UHD Module ends the old clock at a `cold` change's `e₁` when it books the change (UR-25, KC-21a), and `ClockRegistry::end` is set once (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/time/domain.rs:297-322`); a start whose clock began before that `e₁` would leave two clocks of one stream overlapping. MockRadio's start waits the same way, so both start once, after the change, at its value.
- *Never before the origin.* `ClockRegistry::end` accepts an instant before a clock's origin (`domain.rs:297-322`), and a `Stop` can meet a started stream before its first sample, its clock's origin still ahead. MockRadio's `cold_boundary` already waits for the origin (`crates/ezsdr-mock-radio/src/lib.rs:401-405`); the rule now says so for both, so the next start ends that clock at its origin on both Providers, not at the start's instant on one of them.
- *The UHD Module's floor.* With `block_len` up to 65 536, the receive call in progress when a `Stop` arrives can last about 168 ms at 390.6 kS/s, and uhd-rx carries a switch out only once the old stream has ended; a start right after the `Stop` must leave that call before its `e₁`, or its timed start at `e₂` is missed (UR-17: a `radio.LATE_COMMAND` and a first block with `GAP_BEFORE`). Hence UR-25's call-in-progress term, counted from the `Stop`.
- *No lateness.* A start does not act on the device at `at`: the device's timed start is the Provider's own, at `e₂`, the restart lead after `e₁`. Treating an `at` already past as late would turn the host's dispatch delay into a `radio.LATE_COMMAND` — whose default is `mark_artifact` (RM-10) — on a start the Python package timed at the current instant. The new clock's origin records where the stream started.

*Under the pre-release premise:* (d) costs a verb, code in both Providers, a Python method and a package minor, where (a) cost text only; the premise says cost does not decide, and (a)'s way back fails on one Provider. (d), the verb and this timing rule are chosen on their merits.

**Rejected alternatives.**
- *(a) Apply the value and keep the stream stopped, the way back a change of `radio.rx.channels` to 0 and back (draft 1).* Its way back needs a precondition the API cannot state: on the UHD Module the second change must come after the old stream's `e₁`, or the Kernel admits it, the radio refuses it and KC-27 keeps a count no stream has (VERIFIED, the review's probe above); MockRadio accepts it at once. Writing the precondition into RM-16, EA-16 and `_Side.stop`'s docstring would document a Provider difference as the user's recipe, and removing the UHD Module's draining refusal is a parity question of its own (*Follow-up issues*, 1) that (d) does not need. What (a) shares with (d) — the value applies, the stream stays stopped — is kept.
- *(c) Restart, written into the rules (today, in both Providers; UC-3 read literally).* A configuration change undoes a lifecycle Action, `rx.stop()` meaning "until the next `cold` change"; a `hardware_timed` change of the same stream does not restart it; and v3's precursor of `cold` kept the stop.
- *(b) Refuse the update with `radio.COMMAND_REJECTED`.* KC-27 keeps a refused value, so `sdr.rx.sample_rate` would read back a rate the radio refused; a stopped stream could not be configured before it is started; and a retune of a stopped receiver, harmless on a device, would be refused for no device reason.
- *The start as a trigger key, `radio.rx.start` set by `SetParameter` (draft 2).* It works — draft 2's prototype passed — but it is a command written as configuration: KC-27 keeps `radio.rx.start: True` after the first start, so `effective` reports a start while the stream is stopped again; every later update's KA-6 coerce carries it, which needed a `coerce` exception, an RM-4 row and a rule for a Spec that constrains it; and as a `cold` update it fell under the `cold` booking rules (on the UHD Module, UR-25's floor and its refusal of a second change while a switch is pending), and under whatever a device `Stop` decides for pending `cold` changes. It also cannot be timed from a Session.
- *The start as a verb compiled to an `UpdateParameter`.* The Kernel compiles every such verb to an `UpdateParameter` of the bound recorder, not of the resource the verb names (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/session.rs:501-560`), so it would need a Kernel change and still be a configuration write (*Follow-up issues*, 6).
- *A Kernel `Start`.* The Kernel stays small (AGENTS.md §3; Vision §5): one Vocabulary's need is met in that Vocabulary, and a verb meets this one. A generic `Start` would need a meaning for every target a `Stop` reaches — a Sink, whose own `Stop` finishes its capture and keeps it serving requests (HD-11), so it has nothing to resume; an Executor; a radio device, whose transmit side starts with bursts, not with a command; and `Start {}`, for a Run already ending — and it would be a new member of RS-48's closed set, a Kernel major.
- *Rename `PeripheralCommand`, before the freeze, to a name that fits a radio stream.* Not in this spec: the Action's definition is already generic (RS-49: a verb, its params and an optional `at` to any target) and §39 already routes peripheral commands to a radio Provider's sub-resources; a rename touches the Vision's Action set (§5, §19), specs 04 and 06, the Kernel schemas and every Provider's refusal reasons, and fixes nothing #46 has. It belongs to the v4.0 freeze review (*Follow-up issues*, 7).
- *Judge the start when the Provider receives it.* A Spec Run's scheduled start arrives at the start round, before its `Stop` (KC-17, KC-18): "stop at 1 s, start at 2 s" would start nothing. VERIFIED on the prototype: a Spec Run on MockRadio scheduling a `Stop` of `radio/rx` at T0 + 1 ms and `start_rx` at T0 + 3 ms ends the first clock at T0 + 3 ms and starts the second at T0 + 3 ms (`ideal`) or T0 + 53 ms (`x310-like`).
- *At one instant, the `Stop` first (draft 3).* MockRadio handles the Actions it receives in a step before the events due then, so a pending start and a `Stop` at one instant met in that order only when the `Stop` came in the start's own round — a Spec's agenda, at a wakeup (`stepping.rs:419-420`) — and in the other order when it came in a later round, as a Session's does (the review's probe, above: 199 and 149 blocks on a new clock against none); the UHD Module, holding the start to its instant, decided the start first for the same Spec. A rule both Providers could not keep, and under which `rx.start(); rx.stop()` left the stream running.
- *The untimed start at RS-19's command lead (draft 3).* It put the start the radio's command lead (2 ms on `x310-like`, 5 ms on the UHD profiles) after a `Stop` submitted at once after it, so `rx.start(); rx.stop()` left the stream running — draft 3's documented surprise — and the order of two calls depended on the profile.
- *An untimed `PeripheralCommand` at the current instant, in the Kernel (RS-19).* RS-19's lead exists for commands that act on a device at `at`, such as a smart antenna's (§39); a start acts at `e₂`, the Provider's own timed start. The Python package sends the current instant itself, and the Kernel stays as it is.
- *Decide a start that must wait for a `cold` change only once the change is in effect.* Above (*Running from `e`*): on the UHD Module, whose `cold` changes take effect some 55 ms after booking, a `stop()` right after `start()` would come before the start and leave the stream running, on MockRadio after it and leave the stream stopped.
- *A late start, flagged with `radio.LATE_COMMAND`.* Above: the default `mark_artifact` would mark artifacts for the host's dispatch delay, every untimed `rx.start()` on a device-paced Session among them, and the start has no device deadline to miss.
- *The start lead, counted once the old stream has surely ended.* The start is UC-3's restart of the stream the `Stop` ended, not a start from no stream: on the UHD Module the stopped stream's streamer stays open and owned by uhd-rx, which carries the start out as a switch — stop at the cut, reconfigure, timed start — the sequence the restart lead bounds (UR-25), while the start lead bounds a start from a closed streamer that uhd-control opens itself (RM-25's from-zero case). Every profile gives the two leads one value (MR-18's ceiling), so the choice shows only when Phase 8 measures them; then a start after a surely ended stream may take the start lead.
- *Floor a start on the UHD Module at UR-25's whole floor — now plus the restart lead, the call in progress and 3 ms.* The restart lead is already between `e₁` and `e₂`; the whole floor would start the UHD Module some 55 ms later than MockRadio for no device reason. The term the start needs is the call in progress, counted from the `Stop` whose receive call it must wait out (above).
- *End the clock a `Stop` meets before its first sample at its origin when the `Stop` comes (the review's NON-BLOCKING 3, "fix if natural").* It would make a `Stop` end a clock only when the clock carried no sample, so that `ended_at` meant "the next start or change ended it" for one clock and "a `Stop` found it empty" for another; whether a `Stop` ends the receive clock at all is the question rejected below. The next start or change ends that clock at its origin on both Providers (*Never before an origin*), and until then the Manifest shows it without an end on MockRadio, and on the UHD Module when uhd-rx had carried the switch out (*Follow-up issues*, 5).
- *`Tx.start()` beside `Rx.start()`.* A `Stop` does not end the transmit stream (VERIFIED above): its next burst, `tx.repeat` or a `send`, is its start, and a `Tx.start()` would have nothing to start.
- *Leave a device `Stop` cancelling a booked `cold` change on MockRadio.* After `rx.start()`, MockRadio would run at a rate the Kernel does not hold and the UHD Module at the one it holds (VERIFIED today, above).
- *A device `Stop` that disarms the faults, as `stop` does.* The faults are the BindingProfile's environment (Vision §8), not commands to the device; a `Stop` of `<device>/rx` never disarmed them; and the UHD Module keeps checking its device after a device `Stop` (`control.rs:159-162`). #46 does not need the choice; it follows from no longer running the whole `stop`, and it is made here deliberately rather than as a side effect.
- *Apply the value and leave the stopped stream as the `Stop` left it — no cut at `e₁`, no clock ended.* The UHD Module books a switch, with its clock bookkeeping, before it can know of a later `Stop` (UR-25), and cuts at the earlier of `e₁` and the `Stop`'s end (`rx.rs:198-207`); MockRadio would differ from it for a change received before the `Stop`, and a later start would leave two receive clocks without `ended_at` in the Manifest.
- *A stop that lasts for the rest of the Run, even through a count of 0.* An exception to RM-21's "from 0 starts it" and a flag that outlives the stream, for no gain.
- *A `Stop` also ends the receive SampleClock at the stream's end, so that `ended_at` is exact.* Outside #46: no stop ends a clock today (TM-13c sets `ended_at` at a rate change), the UHD Module knows the `Stop`'s end only in uhd-rx, and `ClockRegistry::end` is set once, so a later abort could not correct it.

## KI-1 — UC-3 and TM-13c: a `cold` change restarts only a function it stops (#46; with VG-2)

**Problem.** UC-3 says the target "stops the function the key belongs to at the effective instant, applies the value and restarts it" (`design/05-module-api.md:183`; its copy in spec 06 §10, `design/06-kernel-coordinator.md:542`, is identical, VERIFIED by `diff`). It does not say what happens to a function that a `Stop` has already ended; read literally, "restarts it" starts it again, which is what both radio Providers do (VG-2). The same reading is in two more places: TM-13c, "A sample-rate change is a `cold` update that sets `ended_at` on the current SampleClock and allocates a new id" (`design/01-time-model.md:146`), and `UpdateClass::Cold`'s doc comment, "The target stops the affected function, applies the value and restarts it; a stream continues on a new SampleClock (UC-3, TM-13c)" (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/module_api.rs:411-412`; KA-10's table, `design/06-kernel-coordinator.md:206`), which is the description of `cold` in five committed schemas (`action`, `action_template`, `component_descriptor`, `experiment_spec`, `vocabulary_descriptor`; VERIFIED: regenerating changes exactly these). VG-2 decides the case for radio streams; UC-3 is where every target — Provider, Sink, Executor that applies Actions — reads `cold`, and left as they are, the three texts would contradict RM-16 as VG-2 amends it.

**Decision for the owner.** Taken with VG-2 (*How to decide*).

**Recommendation.** UC-3's first sentence, in spec 05 and in its copy in spec 06 §10 (replaced):

> `cold`: the target stops the function the key belongs to at the effective instant, applies the value and restarts it. A function a `Stop` has ended, or is ending, is not restarted: the change applies the value and, for a stream, ends the stream and its SampleClock at `e₁` if they still run then and starts no new clock; the function starts again only as its Vocabulary says (for a radio receive stream, RM-16) (spec 21, KI-1; issue #46).

UC-3's *Producer obligation* note gains: "and, for a function a `Stop` has ended, by `mr_25_a_stopped_receive_stream_is_not_restarted` and spec 18's `ur_26_a_stopped_receive_stream_is_not_restarted` (spec 21, KI-1)". The rest of UC-3 — the restarted stream's `e₁` and `e₂`, the start from no stream — stays.

TM-13c (sentence added after its first): "A rate change of a stream a `Stop` has ended sets `ended_at`, if the clock has none, and allocates no id: the stream has no clock until it starts again, and its start allocates one (UC-3; spec 21, KI-1; issue #46)."

`UpdateClass::Cold`'s doc comment, and KA-10's row for it in spec 06: "The target stops the affected function, applies the value and restarts it, unless a `Stop` has ended it; a stream continues on a new SampleClock (UC-3, TM-13c)." The five schemas are regenerated (`EZSDR_UPDATE_SCHEMAS=1 cargo test --test schema_freeze`), one description each, and `schemas/SCHEMA_CHANGELOG.md` gains, above spec 20's entries:

> ## v1 — spec 21 — a `cold` change and a stopped function
>
> Still version 1: v4.0 has not frozen. Descriptions only: in `action`, `action_template`, `component_descriptor`, `experiment_spec` and `vocabulary_descriptor`, the description of `UpdateClass`'s `cold` says that a function a `Stop` has ended is not restarted (spec 21, KI-1; UC-3). No field, type or constraint changed.

The Status rows of specs 01, 05 and 06 name the amendment.

**Rejected alternatives.**
- *Leave UC-3 and state the rule in the radio Vocabulary only.* UC-3's literal "restarts it", which both Providers implement today, would contradict RM-16: two accepted specs saying opposite things (spec 20, KH-1's objection).
- *Amend UC-3 and leave TM-13c and the doc comment* (draft 1). TM-13c would still say that every rate change allocates an id, and five schemas would describe `cold` by the reading KI-1 removes; KA-10 made the five descriptions say what each class means, and they should keep saying it. The cost is five regenerated descriptions, still version 1, as KA-10's own change was ("descriptions only", `schemas/SCHEMA_CHANGELOG.md`).
- *Name the radio verb in UC-3 (draft 2).* A Kernel spec would name a Vocabulary's word; UC-3 points to the Vocabulary's rule instead.
- *With VG-2's (c):* UC-3 would instead gain "whether or not a `Stop` has ended it" — rejected with (c).

[#47](https://github.com/k3komatsu/Ez-SDRv4/issues/47) — a `cold` change whose `e₁` falls inside an orderly `Stop`'s tail leaves a fault row with a larger loss than MR-20a's — does not disappear. Its probe, rerun on today's tree and on the prototypes of VG-1 and VG-2 (VERIFIED):

- *What VG-2 removes:* the side effect #47 notes in passing ("どのケースでも `Stop` の後の cold change が新しい SampleClock を登録し、受信を再開しています"). On the prototypes the issue's cases register no second receive clock and deliver nothing after `e₁` (the last block ends at the fault's `k_f`, 1 200 or 800); the one clock ends at `e₁`.
- *What stays:* the defect itself. The four rows are 800, 1 200, 800 and 1 200 on both trees, where MR-20a gives 300, 700, 300 and 700; the boundary cases (the change after the tail: 800 and 1 200; `ideal`: 200) are MR-20a's on both. VG-2 keeps the `cold` change's cut at `e₁` inside the tail — it is what the UHD Module does for a change booked before the `Stop` — and that cut is the earlier end the row was not settled against.
- *What VG-1 and VG-2 add to #47's statement:* "an end moves only earlier" names the other ways a tail's end moves earlier, and they over-count the same way. On `x310-like`, a sequence error at T0 + 0.8 ms, the `Stop` at T0 + 1 ms and `stop(Abort)` at T0 + 1.5 ms record `lost` 1 200, where MR-20a with the abort's end gives 700; with `device_lost` at T0 + 1.5 ms instead of the abort, 1 200; an overrun at T0 + 1.2 ms and the abort, 800 for 300 (both trees). VG-2's start does the same: `start_rx` at T0 + 1.5 ms, inside the tail, records 1 200 for the sequence error and 800 for the overrun (prototype). VG-1 and VG-2 do not cause the abort and loss cases (an abort already moves the end earlier today); they make the end those rows violate a rule.
- *What remains to fix in #47:* a loss settled at an orderly `Stop` against its tail's end must be settled — or settled again — against the end the stream actually has when a `cold` change's `e₁`, a start's `e₁`, an abort or a loss ends it earlier; under VG-1 that end is the earliest of them. #47's own first option, deferring the settlement until the tail has run or the stream ends earlier, covers all four. Not resolved here.

## #48 after this spec

[#48](https://github.com/k3komatsu/Ez-SDRv4/issues/48) asks that MockRadio's ordered loop be written down as it is: each item by the instant at which it does its work, then its effective instant, then insertion — a `cold` change at `(e₁, e, insertion)`, a fault or a held burst at `(instant, instant, insertion)`. VG-2 adds one item to that loop and leaves the question to #48:

- MockRadio holds a pending `start_rx` as a receive `cold` command (MR-25 as amended): its instant of work is the first lattice instant at or after its `e` and the receive floors — the start's `e₁` while the stream's clock runs —, its effective instant its `e`, and its insertion its arrival. Whatever #48 decides for a `cold` change applies to it unchanged: the prototype orders it by `(e₁, e, insertion)`, today's order.
- A `Stop` is not a loop item: it is an Action, handled before the loop, and VG-2 orders it against the starts by deciding first the starts whose `e` has come (RM-16 as amended). #48 does not touch that order.
- A device loss between a start's `e` and its `e₁` — under a sample period unless the clock has not begun or the start waits for a `cold` change — is ordered as #48 orders a `cold` change against a loss: MockRadio applies the loss first and records the start in `rejected`, while the UHD Module, which booked the start at `e`, records nothing. Only the record differs; it is #48's.

8. *Bench: a `Stop` between a start's `e₁` and `e₂`.* uhd-rx carries a start's switch out at once on a stopped stream and issues the timed start at `e₂`; a `Stop` before `e₂` cuts the new stream before its first sample, while the device keeps the queued timed start (VG-2's Evidence, INFERRED). 9. *The UHD Module's transmit `e₁` has no origin floor.* VG-2 floors a receive `e₁` at the old clock's origin on both Providers; MockRadio floors a transmit one too (`cold_boundary`, `lib.rs:396-408`), the UHD Module does not (`book_cold`'s `lattice(i128::from(e), old.n)`, `control.rs:437`), so a transmit enable timed more than the restart lead ahead and a rate change before its origin end the transmit clock before its origin on the UHD Module (INFERRED, by reading; today's code, which VG-2 leaves for transmit). Kept out of this spec: the code fix is one line, but it changes RM-25 and UR-25 for both directions, needs its own transmit test and a probe of a defect so far only read, and #46 does not need it.
