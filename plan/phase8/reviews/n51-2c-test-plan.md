### 1.1 The predicate, pure (`tests/common/iii.rs`; unit tests run in the `fake` binary like every other `iii` test)


`late_evidence` is `Some` when either of: (1) `time_errors` holds a pair whose cause is `"late"` **and whose outcome is not `"late_at_device"`** (a `radio.TIME_ERROR` the Provider reported as host lateness, at receipt or at dispatch: outcomes `send_asap`, `drop`, `plan_violation`, `refused`; the UHD Provider emits only cause `late`: `tx.rs:914`, `control.rs:463`; `unclosed_burst` is not evidence); (2) a `Booked.stamp` (the Action's `LogEntry.time`, the Kernel's stamp at `submit`'s entry: `pipeline.rs:939`, V) is `> deadline`. Strict `>`: equality is on time (`control.rs:491` accepts `T − receipt ≥ lead`). Late side only: no lower bound exists, because a host delay can only add time (V: `Stamped` queue, `rig.rs`; manual clock).

*Why `late_at_device` is not evidence (decision 1; the working tree's §5.3b says so).* The outcome means the Provider handed the burst over **in time by its own clock** and the device reported it late (`tx.rs:1164`, `TimeErrorOutcome::LateAtDevice`, `ezsdr-radio/src/lib.rs:682-684`; V). On the manual clock a descheduled uhd-tx cannot produce it (time stands while a participant is busy), so it points at a hand-over regression, the class a rehearsal should fail on. It is therefore **judged**: a trial whose only TIME_ERROR is `(late, late_at_device)` has no evidence, runs its closure, and fails unmarked (S-1b).

*Why the two clauses are sound.* A receipt lies in `[stamp, clock at submit's return]` (V: `plan/phase8/51-tables.md:51`, from `paced.rs:177-206`), so `stamp > T − lead` ⇒ `receipt > T − lead` ⇒ late at receipt, with no false positive. A TIME_ERROR with an evidence outcome is by construction a late receipt or a late dispatch, both host lateness the spec allows (RM-15). The type has no field for faults, refusals or sends: **the predicate cannot produce a verdict, only withhold one** (P1 as amended in §9).

*A test's own read before `submit` is no clause (decision 2).* The Kernel's stamp is taken at `submit`'s entry, after the test's own `now()` read, so `read > deadline ⇒ stamp > deadline` wherever the trial keeps the Action's `LogEntry`, and clause 2 already covers it; v3's clause 3, `Booked.read`, S-3 and rows 06–07 are removed, and §5.3b's third bullet is no longer in the note's text. Planned instants built from an earlier read (`capture_at`, `tx_at`) stay planning values, not evidence (the note's own caveat).

### 1.3 The marker path and the classification (`common/mod.rs`, next to `rehearse_session_loopback`)
