### The defect
Issue #103 (`gh issue view 103 -R k3komatsu/Ez-SDRv4 --json body --jq .body`, run from /tmp). KC-24a (`design/06-kernel-coordinator.md:329`) says a control-path call holds the admission lock from its first admission through its last dispatch, the start's batch included. `Run::start` path (`crates/ezsdr-kernel/src/coordinator/pipeline.rs:~314-345`): `admit_schedule` (`:804`, no lock; a working copy of the configuration) → `start_instances` → `move_to(Running)` → `paced::start_data_thread` (device-paced) → only then `lock(&shared.admission)` around the dispatch → `paced::wait_finished` → a round. Between `move_to(Running)` and the lock, a Module submission (`pipeline.rs:~1193`, MA-14a; e.g. an Executor's first `step` on the data thread) passes KC-24 step 2 (`Running`), is admitted against the pre-batch configuration and dispatched before the batch; the batch then overwrites it. Expected: entries are dispatched in admission order and each is judged against the configuration the previous one left, so the later-admitted entry stands.

### Direction (the issue's; check it, do not take it on trust)
Take the admission lock after `start_instances()` and before `move_to(Running)`; hold it through the batch's dispatch; release it before `wait_finished` and the round (KC-24a: never across a round or KC-21a's wait). Not before `start_instances`: the mutex is not reentrant, and a submission from inside `start` would block on it (such a submission is refused at KC-24 step 2 anyway, since the Run is not yet `Running` — `admission.rs:44`). Before `move_to(Running)` no Session or Module submission passes step 2, so the window is closed. If you find this direction wrong or incomplete, say why with a sequence and propose the alternative; a change of any rule text or a user-visible behaviour is the orchestrator's to decide (say so; do not assume).
