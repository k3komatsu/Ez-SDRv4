# Design note — #110 under `mech:pending-provenance-collapsed` (AGENTS §6; 2026-11-20, orchestrator)

**Classification.** #110: the start's admitted schedule batch lives in a local of `pipeline()` (pending Actions held apart from the record that must carry them — `termination.undelivered`) and is not handed over on the three early returns between `admit_schedule` and dispatch, so it is lost. That is `pending-provenance-collapsed` (ledger: 5 closed instances, #18 #19 #34 #36 #66; "the next bug needs a note before its fix"). #110 is the 6th.

**The question.** Can the concept be removed or merged, is a rule implemented twice? The pending Actions already have one owner and one finisher for every other origin: the undelivered queues in `Shared`, drained once by RS-6 step 1 (`freeze_held`), which records `undelivered`. The start batch is the one holder outside that owner. The ledger's candidate remedy — "the recording owns its pending state and one finish function consumes it" — applies directly.

**Decision.** Merge, don't patch paths: the admitted batch moves into `Shared` (one more pending holder, `start_batch`) the moment its ids are assigned (at admission, owner 2026-11-19), and the same `freeze_held` that drains the queues drains it into `undelivered`. No early-return path records anything itself (rejected: three per-path recordings; a Drop guard — a second finisher). The dispatch loop takes the batch out of `start_batch` under the admission lock before delivering, so an Action is either delivered or drained, never both or neither. Pinned by tests on each early return (end after admission, after `start_instances`, after `Running`, and after a failed `start()`), each asserting the batch's ids in `undelivered`, and a test that a delivered batch is not also in `undelivered`.

**#113** is `radio-rule-implemented-twice` (MockRadio's receive cut used the stop instant where its transmit cut and the registered-clock path used the origin: two paths inside one Provider). That mechanism's answer (one rule both Providers apply, decided in `ezsdr_radio`) stands; #113 aligns the stray path to RM-25/MR-25, and the UHD Module has no such row (no change).
