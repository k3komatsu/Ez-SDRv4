## Part A review (sol-e2bA-code.md, REVISE: 1 × P1, 1 × P2); 241/241 E1/E2 rows killed remotely after Part A
- P1 a freed slot can reserve a value whose instant has passed (the shared reservation rule checks progress against the cut, not the value's instant): adjudicated (orchestrator). The retry keeps the existing reservation rule, so a held value retried late is treated exactly as a release that arrives late today. No new disposition is added in the experiment; the measurement counts it as a failure (not applied at e). The conflict was in the orchestrator's review prompt ("no value reserved after its instant"), not the brief ("as reserve would report it today"). Open design question for M1 and E3: whether a released value whose instant passed while held should be refused instead (production has no in-flight bound; its booking floor moves a late booking, control.rs:641-673).
