## Orchestrator's rulings (they bind the plan)

**R1 — one receipt-time booking rule, written once, with one invariant.** Whether a `TxBurst` is accepted is decided at its receipt by one pure rule in `ezsdr_radio` (beside `Busy`; extend or replace it — one representation of a stream's accepted bookings serves both this rule and stage 4's value staging), which MockRadio and the UHD Module only call. **Invariant: a booking accepted at receipt plays at its (possibly policy-moved) start on the Module unless the host is late**; so the rule is at least as strict as the Module's device reality, and the Module no longer drops or late-flags at dispatch a booking that receipt accepted, except for host lateness. The rule:
- each accepted, uncancelled burst occupies an interval; a new burst overlapping any of them is refused (`radio.COMMAND_REJECTED`, its id), **in either receipt order** (B received before A is judged as A before B);
- a finite burst's exact end may be continued by a contiguous burst when the Module can continue its device burst there (today: a burst of more than 32 samples); a burst of at most 32 samples is closed on the device, so its interval extends to its padded device end plus the gap the device needs (derive it from S1/S3 and the bench rows: today front = start + 5 for 1–3 samples, end + 1 for 4–32), and a booking inside it is refused (this replaces KL-36's "decided late" by "refused at receipt");
- after a finite burst of more than 32 samples that is **not** continued, a booking in the gap the device needs after its close is refused (derive the gap from the Module's close: held tail, the two-sample EOB, padding; D5's "+2" was the Mock's conservative copy of it — state which bound is right and why);
- a repeat occupies `[start, ∞)` until a `Stop` cuts it; after a `Stop` taking effect at `s`, no interval reaches past `s + tx_in_flight_ns` (stage 4's bound);
- `SendAsap` at booking moves the start first, then the overlap is judged on the moved interval (note 47 §2.1 item 7);
- a booking dropped at booking by its late policy occupies nothing.
State the rule's exact numbers with their evidence (VERIFIED with path:line or bench row, or INFERRED), and show for each of #79's sequences what both Providers now answer.

**R2 — preemption goes.** With overlaps refused, nothing cuts a held or open burst at a later booking's start: delete `Tx::preempt`, the held-cut path in `send`/`step` that only preemption reaches, the Mock's preemption front, and their tests and rows (each with evidence that nothing else reaches it). Keep what note 48 §3.4 keeps (`dispatch_start`, `later_bound`, `late_policy_front_outcome`, the safe start after an EOB) only where the invariant still needs it (host lateness, the first send's window wait); otherwise say why it is now dead.

**R3 — the repeat switch, decided at receipt.** `TxBurst { repeat: true }` with the Vocabulary metadata mark `radio.repeat_operation = "switch"` (RM-13's "metadata is empty" becomes "empty or this mark") replaces a **playing** repeat at a period boundary; without the mark, a repeat on a busy stream is refused by R1. The boundary is chosen **at receipt by one pure function** both Providers call: the first `origin + k·N` (N the playing waveform's length) at or after `max(the Kernel's admitted at, receipt + tx_in_flight_ns + device lead)`. It is never moved. The UHD owner installs it before handing any sample at or past the boundary (serialised with its handed cursor, Sol 9); if a host stall has already handed past it, the switch is refused with its id (a host-lateness outcome, as S8 in stage 4). One pending switch per repeat (a second is refused); `Stop`, a loss, `Provider::stop` and an F8 recovery that ends the repeat cancel the pending switch. The new waveform's length is free; with the same length the period stays locked. The record `{action_id, predecessor, selected_at, old_period, new_period, outcome}` goes in the Provider's record section, tied to the dispatched id. Values (stage 4) do not change at a switch (the stream is busy).

**R4 — Session and Python.** The Rust Session verb `switch_repeat` compiles to R3's marked burst. Python: `tx_streams[k].switch(x)` (checks as `repeat`: channel count, finite samples; no value parameters); a second plain `repeat` on a playing stream raises `Rejected` (the tests that repeat twice change by their subject: those meaning "replace" use `switch`, those meaning "after it ends" stop or wait first).
