F3. **Three fixtures carry `rx_start_lead_ns` = 40 ms** (`valid.json`, `mock-only-rejects.json`, `uhd-only-rejects.json`, and `invalid-comparisons.json`; VERIFIED by reading the JSON). F6. **The 8e8 B/s cap flips three expectations of "200 + 50 MS/s is exactly at the cap"**: `channels.rs::rm_32_throughput_cases` (comment and two assertions), `ezsdr-acceptance/tests/v58.rs:1069-1076` and spec 07's test row (`design/07-radio-model.md:423`: "the 200 + 50 sum admitted"). ## 3. Part B — the receive start head (1-a, 1-a(b))

The function: `ezsdr_radio::x3xx::rx_fill_samples(decimation: u64) -> u64`, next to `clip_decimation`. `n_hb = min(v2(D), 3)`; head = `[2, 13, 18, 24][n_hb]`. Callers: the Mock's `publish_rx` channel-mode branch (`lib.rs:608-688`) through a `Profile` accessor that returns 0 for `ideal` (`Profile::decimation` is `None` there), and the Fake's `sample_at` loopback branch (`device.rs:1275-1294`), where `rx_recv` (`:1844`) passes the stream's origin.
