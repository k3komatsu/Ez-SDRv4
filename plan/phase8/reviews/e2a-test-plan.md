### 2.3 The scoped abandon, specified (A4, F3, #80)

`Bookings` gets one function used by the book (name fixed by the plan, the body by the implementer):

`abandon_repeat(&mut self, cursor_k, bound_root) -> AbandonEffect { cancelled_switch: Option<ActionId>, removed_switch_chain: bool }`, and `TxBook::abandon(is_repeat, cursor_k, …)` around it, which also clears `playing` and removes the switch payload from `held` and `unread` (A1). With `c = cursor_k`, `s = c + STOP_FRONT`:

1. `is_repeat == false`: nothing in the record changes (KL-39, RM-15: a finite burst keeps its region). Today `tx.rs:1021-1031`.
2. Pending switch: `boundary >= c` is cancelled (`cancel_switch_from`), as today; `boundary < c` is **applied at `s`** (an installed switch, today's `halt` arm `boundary < stop`, `staging.rs:492`), as today. An applied switch (settled-unwritten, chain starting at or after `c`) is settled `Cancelled` (`cancel_applied`) and its chain removed, **whether its payload is in `held` or is the unsent `open` booking** (today only the `held` case: `tx.rs:998-1007`; the `open` case is the third named change). This rule takes precedence over rule 5 when an applied S starts at or after `s`.
3. Repeat chains: one starting at or after `c` is removed as today (so when `c == r`, R itself: its first send took nothing at `r`, nothing of R reached the device; R's chain goes and the Stop region `[r, s)` is added, today's `halt` behaviour, so a SendAsap in `[r, s)` is moved, not refused, and a booking that fits before `r` is not refused as handed). The footprint rule applies to the **last repeat chain starting before `c`**: `front = s` (set), `join = false`.
4. (Withdrawn after Sol's review, except the `c == r` region of rule 3.) No other region is added: R's front at `s` is the footprint.
5. Chains with `start >= s` (other than an applied switch's, rule 2) are kept with their `front`, `joined` and `join` as they are.
6. `until`: `stop(bound_root)` only when no chain with `start >= s` remains; otherwise unchanged.
7. `playing`: cleared only when `playing.id` is the abandoned repeat or its pending/applied switch (decided by the book, from `Booked::id()`).

Table-test oracle (P3 at E2a-1, extended into P10 at E2a-2b): for every record with no chain at or after `s`, the new function equals today's `cancel_switch_from(c)`, then `StreamState::stop_at(instant(c), 0)` (= `busy.stop(bound)` + `busy.halt(c, s)`), compared by `PartialEq` on `Bookings` (`staging.rs:263`, `derive(PartialEq)`) plus `take_settled()`. Rows (the named differences are cells here): R alone; R with `c == r` (R's first send `Ok(0)`: chain removed, region `[r, s)`; plus the re-check sequence: R timed at now + 9 ms, first send `Ok(0)`, then a timed finite booking at now + 3 ms, 1 ms long, is **accepted** and a SendAsap in `[r, s)` is moved); R + pending **installed** S with `b > c`, `b == c`, `b < c` (the last: applied at `s`); R + applied S with `b == c` and `b > c`, the payload in `held` and as the unsent `open`; R open after a Stop (front already `stop`); finite alone. With a later chain: B finite, B repeat, B behind a short head; each asserts B's chain, its `front`, `until` and `playing` survive.

Three deliberate differences from today, and no others: (a) the removed `retain(start < t)` of every later chain (#80); (b) F3's applied-switch cell (R + applied S with `b == c`, and `c < b < s`): today R keeps front `b` and `[b, s)` is a separate Stop region, so a SendAsap booking there is moved (`region_move`, `staging.rs:430-436`); with `front = s` it is refused at receipt, as in the pending cell. In the **advance-applied** state (device time already at or past `b`) every booking at `b + 1` is late anyway, so there the change alters only the stored record, not any answer; the answers differ only where the switch is applied in the record while device time is before `b + 1`'s lead (R9's recipe); (c) an applied switch whose payload is the unsent `open` booking is now cancelled (row `cancelled`), where today the row stays `applied`. P10 asserts equality with today's output on every cell except these three, and asserts the expected new value on them. Any other differing cell is a finding.
