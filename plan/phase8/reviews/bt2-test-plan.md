## 0. Decisions

1. **The m hook is a Rust-only builder `UhdRadio::with_measurement_resume_margin(ns)`, no cargo feature** (orchestrator, Q1; §2). Like `with_rx_stall` / `with_measurement_timing` (design/18 446–448). The value travels `UhdRadio` → `Core::new` → `Core::resume_margin()` → `tx.rs:1112`. No Spec key, Binding selector or env var reaches it.
2. **The stall is a hook of `ProbeDevice`** over four disjoint send classes (§3), not a copy of `Descheduled`. It is armed after the control's Stop, so a cell's hold never sees the control's sends.
3. **C7 measures where m is the subject and judges contract properties that hold at every m** (§5). FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE are contract or evidence problems at any m. **The tool never attributes a retry to m** (orchestrator): it counts, per cell, the trials, the resumes, the retries and the unsettled trials, and prints for every retry the raw record (cause, the held send, the reports around its start, which term set R). The owner reads m from the retry rates across the m cells, not from a per-trial cause. A trial whose last part played after retries is `RETRIED(r)`, one that ends with nothing heard after a chain of drops is `UNSETTLED(k)`; both are data, neither is a FAIL, and a cell that has either never prints a bare PASS.
4. **A decided trial carries mode-specific fault evidence** (starvation: RF before the stall and an Underflow report; late start: the held send returned after its own time and a late report or `late_at_device`/`expired` row), and every resume is paired with its accepted start of burst, its reports and its RX span (§4.4).
5. **The RF check is a per-period PN correlation** against a zero-mean waveform (§4.3), anchored to the control's onset (an onset power test), checking the first resumed period alone and the period before it, with a contiguous-capture requirement equal to the phase window. On the fake an idle-ramp sample (`sample == ramp(tick)`) is silence.
6. **The drain is two raw bounds per start of burst, never a class.** For each accepted start `A` the tool prints `D_before` (the last report tick below `A`), `D_after` (the first at or after `A`), the report spacing and the signed distances; it does not say a drain "cleared": nothing in bt2 evidences a report-free stretch (§4.4). τ is a signed record with its endpoints and feeds no class.
7. **C2 at 100/200 MS/s leaves bt2** (orchestrator): its rows, texts, rehearsals and bench commands are not in this plan; phase B adds one line to `plan/phase8/bench.md`: C2's 100 and 200 MS/s cells stay out and the late-device-burst rule at those rates stays INFERRED until revisited after C7 has measured the host-to-radio delay on hardware.
8. **Rates: 1 MS/s and 10 MS/s on the bench** (orchestrator, Q2). Trials per cell 10 / 20 / 20 for m = 10 / 1 / 0.2 ms (Q6). The drop-storm cell (S8, Q4) runs at 1 MS/s. `busy` stays out of the `tx_resume` row (Q5).

## 3. The stall wrapper (`ProbeDevice::hold_tx_send`)

Four disjoint send classes (flush, payload start, close and payload continuation are separate inputs of the hold):

```rust
pub enum SendClass { Flush, Close, Start, Continuation }
// Flush: the existing `tx_flush` (untimed, sob && eob, four zeros)  — T3
// Close: eob && !sob && at.is_some() && samples <= 2               — a device burst's two-sample close
// Start: sob (not a Flush)                                          — a device burst's first send
// Continuation: the rest with samples > 0 (not sob)
pub enum TxHold {
    /// The `nth` (1-based) `Start` handed after arming is held `hold` on the inner device's host clock before the device sees it.
    Start { nth: usize, hold: Wall },
    /// The first `Continuation` handed at least `after` (inner clock) after the first `Start` since arming is held `hold`.
    Midplay { after: Wall, hold: Wall },
    /// Every `Start` since arming is held `hold` (S8). Closes, flushes and continuations are never held.
    EveryStart(Wall),
}
```
- `arm(plan: Vec<TxHold>)`: the plan is a list of holds (each `Start` / `Midplay` fires once; S4' arms two `Start` entries, `nth` 1 and 2); nothing before the call counts. `Start { nth }` and `Midplay { after }` count from the arming (the first `Start` after it, on `inner.host().now()` at its `host_before`), so the control's sends in the same Run (its start, its close) neither fire the hold nor start the `after` clock. The cell's trial arms only after the control's Stop is applied (its two-zero `Close` row is in the probe), and before it books the cell's repeat: no control send can arrive after arming, and the cell's start cannot precede it.
- The hold sleeps on `self.inner.host().sleep(d)` **before** `host_before_ns` is read, so a probe row's `before..after` is the inner call alone; each `tx_send` row gains `"class"`, `"held_ns"` (0 when not held) and `"host_inner_before_ns"` / `"host_inner_after_ns"` (`inner.host().now()`; on the manual-clock fake these are the axis the Module's own `core.now()` uses, which `host_ns` (an `Instant`) is not).
- The held send's class, its accepted sample count and which `Start`/`Continuation` it was are recorded in the row; the trial asserts them (INCONCLUSIVE when the stall did not happen where the cell says: not exactly the planned number of holds, wrong class, a `Midplay` continuation of fewer samples than the cap).
- Reuse: one more hook beside `fail_untimed_apply` and `underflow_after_tx_send` in the wrapper both binaries share. `fake.rs`'s `Descheduled` is not copied: it is a descheduled *thread* that stops the manual clock, wrapping a concrete `FakeDevice` (`fake.rs:3769`).
- Tests (`probe_device.rs`'s `#[cfg(all(test, not(feature = "uhd")))]` module, on a manual-clock `FakeDevice`): `probe_send_class_table` (flush; two-sample eob close; three-sample eob continuation is a Continuation; a short closed first send `sob && eob` is a Start; an untimed non-flush SOB is a Start); `probe_hold_start_holds_only_the_nth_start`; `probe_hold_midplay_picks_a_continuation_after_the_play_time` (not the start, not a send before `after`); `probe_hold_every_start_holds_starts_only_never_a_close_a_flush_or_a_continuation`; `probe_hold_counts_only_from_arming` (sends before `arm` are not held and do not start the `after` clock); `probe_hold_time_passes_on_the_inner_clock` (the manual clock advances by the hold); `probe_hold_rows_record_class_held_and_inner_times`.

Mutation rows (each with the fixture that reaches its branch): **C7-10** `if tx_flush {` → `if false {` in `send_class` (fixture: `EveryStart` over a sequence with a flush; killer `probe_hold_every_start_holds_starts_only…`); **C7-11** the Close test `samples <= 2` → `true` (fixture: a three-sample eob continuation; killer `probe_send_class_table`); **C7-12** `Start { nth }` fires on every start (fixture: two starts; killer `probe_hold_start_holds_only_the_nth_start`); **C7-13** `>= after` → `>= Wall::ZERO` in `Midplay` (fixture: a continuation before `after`); **C7-14** `inner.host().sleep(d)` → `std::thread::sleep(d)` (killer `probe_hold_time_passes_on_the_inner_clock`); **C7-15** the arming guard removed (fixture: a start before `arm`); **C7-16** `EveryStart` holds `Start | Close` (fixture: a start then its close; killer asserts `held_ns == 0` on the close row); **C7-17** `held_ns` recorded 0 always.

## 4. The C7 trial (`tests/common/c7.rs`, one body for the fake and the bench)

### 4.1 Cells

- Modes: **(a) late start**: a repeat is booked **6.5 ms ahead** (`BOOK_AHEAD_FULL`) so that at dispatch `avail = 10 − 6.5 = 3.5 ms` covers the cap (2.5 ms at 1 MS/s, 2 ms at 10 MS/s, F11): the first send is a full chunk, `n1 = cap`; `Start { nth: 1, hold: 40 ms }` holds it far past the start. **(b) starved**: a repeat booked 10 ms ahead (the normal short first send), `Midplay { after: 60 ms, hold: 40 ms }`: the continuation is held 40 ms, more than the in-flight window (10 ms) plus a chunk, so the device runs dry; a continuation's size is the cap by F11. The accepted first-send size `n1` of the **held** send is printed per trial (full / short); a short `n1` in (a) is a measured trial, never INCONCLUSIVE, and is counted so that `m_bound` and the short-first trials can be told apart (§4.4). **Admission latency does not shorten `n1`** (F11: a booking that reaches the Module later gets more window); it eats the 1.5 ms above the 5 ms command lead, so the booking turns late, moved or refused. The trial prints the booking's lateness or refusal, and a refused booking is INCONCLUSIVE `booking refused`.
- m ∈ {10, 1, 0.2} ms via the hook. **Rates 1 and 10 MS/s.** Trials per cell: 10 at m = 10 ms, 20 at 1 and 0.2 ms. **One Session (Run, capture, Manifest) per trial**, as every n47s5 trial is. Both boards.
- Waveform (`c7::wave(rate)`): a QPSK PN, **made zero-mean over one period by construction** (mean subtracted, rescaled to peak 0.495 ≤ 0.5), **N = 100 samples at 1 MS/s and N = 1 000 at 10 MS/s: a lattice period of 100 µs at both**, so R's rounding slack (0…100 µs) is below the smallest m (0.2 ms) and the correlation is position-unique. Occupied bandwidth about ±0.5 MHz at 1 MS/s and ±5 MHz at 10 MS/s (the loopback's attenuator is broadband; the owner confirms the PN at both, §7).

### 4.2 One trial

Session on `transmit_run_with(profile, probe, Some(rate), |r| r.with_measurement_resume_margin(m))`: `n47s5::transmit_run` gains an `adjust` argument (the old signature wraps it) and asks the receive rate too (`radio.rx.s0.sample_rate_hz`, as `measurement.rs:2369`; the fake's loopback forwards only equal rates); `record_rows_per_kind` 100 000 in the selector except S8. Times are relative to the capture's start `c0`:
1. A capture of **360 ms** (3.6 M samples at 10 MS/s, 29 MB in `/tmp`, one trial at a time, deleted after analysis) is booked 2 ms ahead; the first 20 ms are the quiet floor.
2. **Control, in the same Run:** `start_repeat` of the PN on time at +25 ms, `Stop` at +80 ms. It gives the loop delay `d`, the control's per-period gain and phase, the floor statistics and the per-period SNR (§4.3). When the control's `Close` row is in the probe the hold is **armed** (≈ +85 ms), then the cell's repeat is booked.
3. **The cell's repeat**: (a) booked at +123.5 ms for a start at +130 ms (the first send held from ≈ +123.5 to +163.5; R about +165…+180); (b) booked at +90 ms for a start at +100 ms (the first start dispatched about +90 ms, so `after` = 60 ms makes the held continuation ≈ +150, held to ≈ +190; the radio runs dry about 10 ms after the last accepted sample ≈ +162; R about +195…+210). `Stop` once the last part has played 30 ms past its `resumes_at` (or at the cell's latest time, +300 ms, when no part is heard by then). Then wait `CAPTURE_WRITTEN`, `finish()`, read the Manifest. All cells fit the 360 ms with slack. A chain of retries advances R by about a chunk + the margin + 2 ms per retry (≈ 5 ms at the default m, ≈ 2.7 ms at 0.2 ms), so up to ~50 rows fit between +165 and +300 ms.
4. Evidence is read from the Manifest and the probe, never from the host clock alone: events (kind, `target`, `resumes_at`), `timing` `tx_resume` rows, stats `tx_resumes` and `tx_gap_samples`, the `async` rows (code, tick), `bursts`, `record_bound.omitted` (read with `section_unchecked` where truncation is the subject, `omitted_rows` otherwise), the capture's `(tick, sample)` blocks, the probe rows (`class`, `held_ns`, `at`, accepted samples, `host_*` times), `judge_end` (the Run's end tick).

### 4.3 The RF detector (F1, F3)

All RF decisions use **period-aligned PN correlation**: for a window of one period starting at tick `t`, `gain(t)` and `phase(t)` are the magnitude and angle of `Σ w*·s` over the period divided by `Σ|w|²` (`w` is zero-mean by construction, so a DC or LO-leakage offset in `s` contributes `mean(s)·Σw* = 0` and the detector needs no mean subtraction of its own; the invariant lives in `c7::wave`, pinned by §9 C7-21). The control gives `g_c` (median of its periods 2…20), `phase_c`, the first-period gain `g_c1` and phase, the floor statistic `q` (the maximum `gain` over the quiet interval's windows at stride N/4, about 800 windows at 1 MS/s), `σ_g` (the rms of the window gains over the quiet interval) and `snr = g_c/σ_g`; and the **power statistics**: `P_c` (the median per-sample power `mean|s|²` of the control's periods 2…20) and `p_q` (the maximum of `mean|s|²` over N/4-sample windows at stride N/4 in the quiet interval). **Numbers (INFERRED, from the one noise record we have):** the quiet receive power per sample was 6.4–8.5e-9 in 30 tone-probe lines (OBX #1, 2026-10-29, `hw_d9_p7_the_current_transmit_shape.log`, 1 MS/s); with `Σ|w|² = N·0.495² = 24.5` at N = 100, `σ_g = √(7e-9/24.5) = 1.7e-5` and `q ≈ 2.6·σ_g = 4.4e-5`; the loop gain is 0.002–0.015 (F8), so `snr` is about 120–880 and `T = max(2q, 0.05·g_c) = 0.05·g_c` over the whole range and `g_c1/T ≈ 20` (14 if the DUC's first period is at 70 % of steady), against the bar 4. For white noise `σ_g` does not depend on the rate; if the per-sample noise is rate-independent it is 3.2× smaller at 10 MS/s: either way the bar is met by a factor ≥ 3.5 (not measured at 10 MS/s or with the PN; the bench prints `snr` and `g_c1/T` per trial, and the first `hw_c7` run is the check). `heard(t) = gain(t) ≥ T`, `T = max(2·q, 0.05·g_c)` (a false `heard` is about 1e-11 a window; a leak below 5 % of the loop gain is not seen by design). The control must be separable: `g_c1 ≥ 4·T`, else INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Idle-ramp baseline (fake only, F1).** On the fake the receive samples outside any transmitted sample are the ramp of F6, not noise: with a baseline of `|tick, s| if s == ramp(tick, ratio) { 0 } else { s }` the quiet floor and every gap window are exactly 0 on a good path (so `q = p_q = 0` there and every threshold above has its term relative to the control: `0.05·g_c`, `0.1·P_c`), while a transmitted sample (which *replaces* the ramp) is kept as is (a plain ramp subtraction would corrupt it). The ramp wraps every 65 536 samples (65 ms at 1 MS/s, 6.5 ms at 10 MS/s) and a wrap inside a window gives a correlation near 0.2 that the baseline removes. The hardware tool uses the identity baseline. The rehearsal's capture gap contains at least one wrap at 1 MS/s (asserted: the test books against `time_now` so that a wrap tick lies in `[from, R)`; at 10 MS/s every gap contains several), so a mutant that drops the baseline false-FAILs deterministically.
- **Loop delay `d` anchored to an observed onset (F3).** `d` = the lag in `[0, N)` maximising `|corr|` of the control's first 20 periods starting at its accepted start `at` (the probe row of the control's start: the device tick it carries). The **onset** is a power test, not a gain test: the first N/4-sample window (stride N/4) with `mean|s|² ≥ Tp`, `Tp = max(4·p_q, 0.1·P_c)`; a partly overlapped window holds the fraction `f` of the steady power, so the test fires once `f ≥ 0.1`, within N/4 of the true onset whatever the correlation lag (a gain test on a partial window picks up PN sidelobes of order `g_c·√f/√N` that exceed `T` for `f > 0.25` and put the onset 0.25–0.75 N early). The onset must lie within half a period of `at + d`, else INCONCLUSIVE `the loop delay is not resolved against the control's onset` (a periodic PN ties peaks a whole period apart, so a correlation argmax alone cannot place `d` mod N). `d ≥ N` is INCONCLUSIVE (INFERRED: 44 samples at 1 MS/s per `tone.rs:~350`; not read at 10 MS/s).
- **Per-period checks at a played part (start `A = A_j`):** (P0) `W0 = [A+d, A+d+N)` alone: `gain ≥ 0.5·g_c1` and `|phase − phase_c1| ≤ 15°` (the control's first period shares its DUC transient); (P−1) `W−1 = [A+d−N, A+d)` is **not** heard; (P3) periods 2…20 each `gain ≥ 0.5·g_c`, phases within 15° of `phase_c`; (P4, diagnostic) the argmax of the 20-period correlation over lags `−2…+2` samples around `A+d` is at 0, else the line names the offset. **If the control's first period is not separable from the floor (`g_c1 < 4·T`) the trial is INCONCLUSIVE** (INFERRED for both rates, numbers above; `snr` and `g_c1/T` are printed per trial). The phase reference is the control's, from another device burst about 100–150 ms earlier, where every existing phase check compares within one burst: INFERRED that the loop's phase is stable across two bursts of one Run. Mode (b) also prints the part's phase against the **pre-stall periods of the same repeat** (`phase_vs_prestall_deg`, a cross-check, not a decision): if the control's reference failed, every trial of a cell would show the same offset there.
- **Windows and coverage.** The silence windows lie **wholly inside** the silence region: a window `[t, t+N)` is examined only when `t ≥ from_1 + d + 2N` and `t + N ≤ E`, `E` the region's end (the part's `A_j + d`, or for a chain that ends with nothing heard `min(capture end, judge_end + d)`); a window straddling `E` would pick up the part's PN at a non-zero lag. The capture must be contiguous (no hole; `mt3::covers`) over `[from_1 + d − N, E)` in every case, and in addition over `[A_j + d − N, A_j + d + 20N)` for a played last part, else INCONCLUSIVE `hole in the coverage`. Coverage = the phase window for a played part and the whole silence region for a chain without one.
- **Silence** in `[from_1 + d + 2N, E)` over every gap (a dropped part transmits nothing, so the region is continuous when every part before the last is dropped): any examined window with `heard` is `SilenceViolated`. `from_1`: in (a) the held send's `at` (nothing of the dropped burst plays); in (b) the first row's `from` (`H`, the Module's handed cursor). This is conservative: the held continuation lies inside `H` and is dropped (F3), so RF actually stops at the starvation point before `H`; the interval from the starvation to `H` is not checked, and the printed `gap_us = R − H` understates the silence. The trial also prints `rf_end_us` (the end of the last `heard` period before the stall, from the capture) so the true silent span is read.

### 4.4 Evidence, pairing, retries, drain, binding term

- **Pairing.** Each `tx_resume` row `i` (1-based, in order) pairs with its accepted payload `Start` probe row `S_i` (the first `sob`, non-flush send with `at ≥ row.resumes_at`, on the lattice, after the row's close; `A_i = S_i.at`; `moved = A_i > R_i`, printed). A row's state is **paired** (a `Start` exists), **open** (no `Start`, and the Run ended less than 5 ms past `R_i`: the Stop came first, the part was not due), or **unpaired** (no `Start` although the Run ran ≥ 5 ms past `R_i`: INCONCLUSIVE `no start after resume`, §5 reason 11). A `Start` after the first drop that matches no row is INCONCLUSIVE `unpaired start`. A `Start` is **open** when `A_i + 5 ms > judge_end` (the part had no time to play: 5 ms covers a Module poll and a report's delivery, INFERRED; the part's drop, if any, is not yet visible). Judgements are made at `A_i`, never at the planned `R_i`. With complete rows (`omitted` empty): `stats.tx_resumes == rows` and `stats.tx_gap_samples == Σ(resumes_at − from)` (FAIL `CountersDisagree` otherwise: these are the Module's own counters, F5).
- **Drop evidence for a start.** `S_i` is **dropped** when `W0_i = [A_i+d, A_i+d+N)` is **not heard** (the capture, inside the coverage of §4.3) and a TimeError `async` report with tick `≥ A_i − 2 ms` exists (F17: the tick relation is INFERRED on the X310; the 2 ms slack is wide; a silent start with no such report is not dropped, it is a candidate for `NoResume`), and either row `i+1` exists with cause `late_at_device` or `expired` (`expired` is what a retry gets when its held send returns after the timeline: the S4' case), or, for the last start only, no row `i+1` exists yet (the Run stopped first). A start whose `W0` **was heard** and which is followed by row `i+1`, or a silent start followed by a row of cause `underflow`, is INCONCLUSIVE `a start was followed by another resume that is not its drop` (a new fault the cells do not create; not an m question); a dropped start without a report is INCONCLUSIVE `drop without a report`.
- **Mode-specific fault evidence (a decided trial needs it).** (a) late start: the held send is a `Start` and its mapped return `r − bracket > at` (it demonstrably returned after its own time; a return within the bracket of `at` is INCONCLUSIVE, not late); the first row's cause ∈ {`late_at_device`, `expired`} **and** at least one of: a TimeError report with tick ≥ `at`, the row's cause `late_at_device`. **The first incident of mode (a) resumes as `expired` almost always** (after the held send returns, the next turn finds the timeline passed before any report is read, `tx.rs:468–470` → `timeline_expired`, `tx.rs:1027–1029`): on the fake and the X310 alike, `late_at_device` as a cause appears only on later rows; the bench summary says so, so that nobody reads it as a missing observation. (b) starved: **RF was heard before the stall** (≥ 5 consecutive `heard` periods with phase within 15° of `phase_c`, ending before the held send's mapped `host_before − bracket`) **and an attributable Underflow-class report** (Underflow or UnderflowInPacket in the `async` rows, tick between the held send's mapped `host_before − bracket` and `A_1`; a correct Module may write cause `expired` here, so the cause is `underflow` or `expired`, and the report, not the cause, is the evidence; that the X310 attaches a tick to an Underflow report is INFERRED, the Module's own code relies on it). Missing evidence: INCONCLUSIVE `no attributable <mode> evidence`. A starved cell with no report can no longer end CLEAN. The mapped host times are used in these **two comparisons only** (the held send really late; the starvation report window), each with the bracket in the direction that yields INCONCLUSIVE, never a decision.
- **Retries are counted and described, never attributed.** A **retry** is a row `i ≥ 2`. Per trial the tool counts `rows`, `retries = rows − 1`, and for each retry prints one `C7 retry` line, raw: `cause`, `from`, `resumes_at`, the decision tick `at`, the previous start `A_{i−1}` and whether it was held (`held_ns`), its mapped return minus its time (`return_minus_at_us` ± bracket), `m_bound` and `r_minus_busy_us` of this row (below), and the report record of `A_{i−1}`: `D_before`, `D_after`, the report spacing. A trial prints the first five retry lines and the last in full and counts the rest (a chain on the fake runs to ~50 rows). Nothing in a class reads these fields; a fixture pins that two evidence sets differing only in them give the same class (§9 C7-37). The owner reads m from the retry rates across the m cells.
- **Drain record, raw (F16, F7).** For every paired start `S_i`: over all `async` reports (TimeError, Underflow, UnderflowInPacket) of the trial, `D_before_i = max{tick < A_i}` and `D_after_i = min{tick ≥ A_i}` (a report exactly at `A_i` belongs to `D_after`), `tail_gap_i` (the median gap between the last five consecutive report ticks before `A_i`: about 60 µs, F7, so the resolution is read, not assumed), and the signed distances `A_i − D_before_i` and `D_after_i − A_i` in µs. **No class is derived from them**: a report-free stretch after `D_before` cannot be evidenced (F16: the probe logs no poll, the Manifest carries no host time, the Module's reader cadence is unmeasured), reports may stop before the drain ends (F2: a 32-entry FIFO, a slow reader), and for a dropped part the reports after `A_i` mix the old drain's tail with the part's own drop, which the record cannot separate. So the tool never prints that a drain "cleared", and `A_i − D_before_i` is printed as raw data, with a cell statistic (min/median/max) over the starts whose part played, labelled "distance from the last report before the start; reports may stop before the drain ends (F7)". The value of `D_after_i` for a part that played (a report at or after the start of a part that was not dropped) is counted: `reports_after_a_played_start`.
- **Which term bound R (the lead floor).** From the `tx_resume` row (`at` = the decision instant `now`, `resumes_at` = R): `m_bound = tick(R) − (at + lead) ≥ N·per` (R is then set by `busy + m`, not by `now + lead`; the front is H + 2 + g, far lower). Also printed: `n1` of the held send, `m_eff = A_1 − busy_replay` and `r_minus_busy_us` per retry row (the margin the Module actually gave over its own `busy` bound; `busy_replay` re-runs `busy ← max(busy, r_i, x_i) + n_i·per` over the probe rows with `r_i` the mapped `host_after`, the close included, T3 excluded). Expected where it binds (INFERRED): `busy ≈ r + n1·per + 2`, so `busy + m` beats `now + 2 ms` iff `n1·per + m > 2 ms + δ`, `δ` the delay from the send's return to the decision. At 1 MS/s with a full first send (2.5 ms) m binds for every m ≥ 0 when `δ < 0.5 ms + m`; at 10 MS/s (2 ms) m binds only when `m > δ`; with a **short** first send m binds only when `m > 2 ms − n1·per + δ`. On the fake `δ = 0`; on hardware it may not be at 0.2 ms. The cell line says `m_bound k/n` and `n1_full j/n`; **the m-informative trials of a cell are `n1_full ∧ m_bound`**, printed as `m_informative k/n`. If it is low at m = 1 and 0.2 ms the finding is "m below ≈ 2 ms − chunk time is unobservable on this path: `now + lead` decides": an open question for the orchestrator, not a reason for a second hook.

### 4.5 The independent expected R (the F2 pin)

`c7::expected_r(rows, m, lead, origin, period, rate, mcr) -> i64` re-implements F4 in the test: `busy_replay` over the probe rows (`r_i` from `host_inner_after_ns` mapped through the clock anchor on the fake, through the smallest-RTT anchor on hardware), `unaligned = busy_replay + m`, `decided = row.at + lead`, `front = H + 2 + g`, `R = first lattice point ≥ max(unaligned, decided, front)`. Its pure test (`c7_expected_r_pins_mt3d_numbers`) uses mt3d V5's hand numbers (period 1 000, k0 = 20 030: m = 10 ms → 33 030; period 12 502 → 32 532) and the period-100 table of S1b (998 → 23 530, 999/1 000 → 23 630, 200 → 23 030, 10 000 → 32 630). Every Session rehearsal asserts `row.resumes_at == expected_r(its own probe rows, the test's m)` for **every row**, not only the first: the test's m, not the Module's, so a hook that is ignored fails. The assertion is made against what the Module *did* (the first send's accepted size and return are read from the probe, so the check holds whatever the manual clock's wake order gave) and **the cell fails (not skips) when the accepted size is not the one the shape pins**: `full` shapes assert `n1 == cap`, the `short` shape asserts `32 ≤ n1 < cap`.

### 4.6 The delay record (S6; F5)

τ in addendum §2.3 is the host-to-radio delay of the Module's path in `D ≤ busy + τ + ε·S + b`. Two things are measured and printed; **neither enters a class**:
- **`delta_first = t1 − r1`** (mode (a), first incident): `t1` = the tick of the first TimeError report of the dropped burst (the FPGA's time check of the first *packet*, possibly after its arrival: F12), `r1` = the mapped host time at which the held `tx_send` **returned** (after the last fragment was handed). It is a **signed** number with its endpoints and bracket printed: `delta_first_us = <t1 − r1> (t1 = <tick>, r1 = <tick> ± <bracket>)`. It is **neither a delivery delay nor a bound**: with fragmentation `t1` can precede `r1` (negative value) while the last fragment is still in flight. It is a hardware record for the owner and for the later C2 revisit, and says nothing about m.
- **`m_eff` and the drain record** of §4.4: what m must cover is `τ + ε·S + b` plus the clock-mapping errors, observed through the radio's own reports, with the F7/F6/F16 caveats.
- `map`: an affine host→device map from `ProbeDevice::clock_anchor()` read 3× before and 3× after the trial; the anchor with the smallest round trip is used and its **bracket** (half the round trip) is printed per trial (INFERRED 0.05–0.15 ms, F10). The bracket is printed next to every mapped quantity.
- Resolutions, per rate: the heard edge is one period (100 µs at both rates); the report spacing is about 60 µs at both rates (F7, VERIFIED for 20 ms bursts, INFERRED for C7's shape), printed per trial as `tail_gap_us`; `m_bound` one lattice period (100 µs); the mapped `r` ± the bracket.

The `hw_c7_*` tests fail on any FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE, at the end and after every cell's lines are printed (as the other bench tools); `RETRIED` and `UNSETTLED` do not fail them. Every decision line is printed whatever the outcome.

**Where a false PASS or false FAIL on an X310 would come from, and what the plan does about it.** A capture hole hiding a sound in the gap (a false silence): coverage reason, not the power test alone. A restart one period late with the right phase (false CLEAN): `W0` alone and `W−1` (rows 8–10), coverage = phase window. A ramp wrap or DC step read as PN (false SilenceViolated on the fake): baseline; on hardware a zero-mean `w` and a `2·q` floor. A window straddling the part's start (false SilenceViolated): only windows wholly inside the region are examined. A wrong onset (false INCONCLUSIVE, or a wrong `d` read as phase drift): the power test and the half-period check. LO drift between the control and the part within one Run (false PhaseDiscontinuous): a spread of the cell's phases is printed, and mode (b) prints the same repeat's pre-stall phase; the 15° gate is the existing `phase_is_steady` bar (INFERRED small over about 300 ms). A starved cell with no starvation (false CLEAN): the evidence requirement. A repeat that never plays after a chain of drops on an X310 (a possible false PASS of the hardware test): it is `UNSETTLED` by design, a datum, and the cell's `PASS (contract); … unsettled in u/n` line carries it; it is the owner's reading, not the tool's FAIL. A slow Module reader that delays a drop's report beyond 5 ms (a false `NoResume` FAIL on a silent part that was dropped but not yet read): the open margin is INFERRED, the trial line prints the start, the reports and `judge_end`, and the case is read from them; it is not hidden. A drain that "ended" because the reports stopped: the tool does not say so (§4.4).
