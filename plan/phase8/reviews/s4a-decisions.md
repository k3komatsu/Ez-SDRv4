- 1-a (owner 2026-11-17, "はい，これでよいです"; replaces direction D's zero-count bound 0/4/11/15 and the pre-registered fallback B): the receive start head is modelled by its settling length, not by its exact-zero count. Per DDC half-band class n_hb = min(v2(D), 3), the samples until the magnitude first reaches 90 % of steady (zeros + ramp90) are 0–2 / 12–13 / 17–18 / 24 on both boards over all 20 rate cells, gain 0/+10/+20 dB and every start kind (bench 2026-11-16, hw_rxzeros_sweep); the bound is 2 / 13 / 18 / 24, and MockRadio and FakeDevice deliver that many zeros at every receive start (one shared function in ezsdr_radio). The exact-zero count itself falls with the received level (100 MS/s: 3–4 at +0 dB, 0–1 at +20 dB; 1 MS/s: 14–15 → 9–10), so it is no bound. Every start kind (fresh open, retune, after a rate change) is within ±1 of a restart: the head applies to every start. PN: the signal after the head is on time (the zeros replace samples). The 90 % threshold is the orchestrator's choice; a 99 % length is computable from the trials' 40-sample head dumps (not done).
