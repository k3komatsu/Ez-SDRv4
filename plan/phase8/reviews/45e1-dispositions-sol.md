# Sol's E1 code review: the orchestrator's dispositions (2026-11-02)

Verdict REVISE: six P1 and two P2 findings, plus notes. All are adopted. Where Sol's fix leaves a choice, the orchestrator's decision is given below.

| # | Sev | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 | `retire_unread` forgets unread left values, so a Restore becomes falsely eligible | **Adopted.** Verified: production keeps them. `report_debt` moves debt into `left` (provider/core.rs:367-370), and `restore_rx` skips the restore while debt or left is on the channel (rx.rs:443-451). The orchestrator's earlier "retire after flush" (draft 7) was wrong and is withdrawn. Decision: unread leftovers stay `left` in the cell until consumed or lost. A per-cell "flush attempted for this segment end" fact stops repeated flushes. A Restore whose channel still holds left after the flush was attempted is `Never`, a skipped restore as in production. Before that, it waits. Retarget E1-107 and E1-118 and their tests. |
| 2 | P1 | Sending Close does not close acceptance; a reservation can pin the chain before R runs Close | **Adopted.** Note 45 §4.5 requires it. Decision: Close and Open are decided only when the chain is not accepting (phase `Off`, `Uncertain` or `Lost`→`Never`; not `Armed`/`Streaming`/`Stopping`/`Flushing`) and not busy. `reserve` already refuses any phase but `Armed`/`Streaming`, so acceptance is closed by construction. Sol's sequence becomes a regression test. Check the oracle side: I3 at Close's execution as well as at its routing. |
| 3 | P1 | A simulated brain panic does not kill the brain | **Adopted.** After the guard's four operations the brain is dead: `BStep`/`BOp` are disabled for good, and its inbox is never served. The executors exit on F at their checks (already). Post-panic accounting is the draft-5 P2 rule. Case 6 must pass with a dead brain. |
| 4 | P1 | A restart queued behind a stopped tail never starts (`then` is never promoted) | **Adopted.** Model FakeDevice's tail-to-next-start transition (device.rs:1627-1640), including its late-start behaviour. Extend U15d to deliver the next segment's samples. |
| 5 | P1 | Consumption identities are rebuilt from the reservation, not from observed device facts; Move changes cell ownership only | **Adopted.** The applied `Ident` takes its channel from the DSP chain the device actually applied to (the `dsp` map key), and the evidence `Ident` its channel from the receiving streamer's actual channels. I4 compares these observations with the reservation. Consumption uses the streamer's channels (device), not the cell's. A Move's channel change reaches the device only through the streamer transition: the stream's next Open uses the cell's channel set. |
| 6 | P1 | Full case 7b has no `call_faults`; the sweeps never require their witnesses | **Adopted.** Every variant whose plan branch needs a call fault enables it. A walk sweep fails (`Vacuous`) when a declared witness is never reached over its seeds. Bounded micro searches stay report-only for witnesses (owner decision). |
| 7 | P2 | The key merges states whose future witnesses differ (`shortened`, witness bits read later) | **Adopted.** Every piece of witness state that a later witness check reads goes back into the key (`shortened`, the "waited" markers, any witness bit read by `witness_before`/`witness_issued`). Fix `harness_key_ignores_history` accordingly. |
| 8 | P2 | At the epilogue end, an early synthetic timer counts as a deadline observation | **Adopted.** It withdraws draft 5 (iii). After the epilogue end the device produces no samples, but time continues: a silent `Tick` is enabled only while a timer or recv deadline lies beyond `T`. Timers and recv timeouts fire at their real time, and no early delivery happens. R still starts no new recv cycle after the end. |
| n1 | note | Hand-written DFS loops in world.rs tests bypass the explorer | **Adopted (minor).** Replace them with `dst::explore`/`assert_bounded` and focused checks. |
| n2 | note | Several rows reinforce model simplifications | Covered by 1, 5 and 8: those rows are retargeted to production semantics. |
