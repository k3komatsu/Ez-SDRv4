# #75 — Fable 5.2 design review of option 2 (2026-11-13; claude-fable-5-2 subagent, ~144k tokens, read-only, no build)

Verdict: sound with changes; no sequence where a retry itself harms the device (a second untimed EOB is idempotent, P5 C bench-results.md:221; a new streamer's init flushes the endpoint ingress and resets flow control, chdr_stream_input.v:346-381, VERIFIED).
- F1 (major): "confirmed" is not tied to the attempt's flush: the radio posts the ACK to its input graph edge, not a streamer generation (UHD 4.10 radio_control_impl.cpp:1312-1317, VERIFIED); the flush accepts any BurstAck on an expected position even after a send that put nothing out (core.rs:1231-1260). Sequence A: close reports failure but its EOB was delivered; reopen; T3 send returns 0; the old ACK arrives → confirmed with no flush sent. Sequence B: front from the old burst's end while T3 plays at about e..e+4; a held burst at e+g sits 11 samples behind the flush at 1 MS/s (INFERRED; exists today). Change: an attempt is confirmed only if its send was whole and every position ACKed; front at the largest ACK tick read (or ACK end + 4 + g); drop S9 or replace it with a foreign-ACK test.
- F2 (owner decision, decided: one COMMAND_REJECTED only at the final failure; a row per attempt): `device_failed(None, "tx_flush")` at core.rs:1235 emits per failed send.
- F3 (major for decision 3): no two-channel flush ACK on hardware (213 pairable rows, all channel 0); 1–2 MS/s four-sample untimed burst 3 trials per cell; two-sample form underflows there (bench-results.md:327-331); 0 misses in 241 open flushes → ~1 % per-attempt upper bound. (3) gated on the 2026-11-15 sweep (owner).
- F4 (major, placement): the loop in `open_tx_device_locked` (both callers: arm via open_streamer core.rs:1385, and reopen_tx_streamer); `Ok` means confirmed (remove `acknowledged` from the success value so tx.rs:816/821-824 cannot collapse it); check stop/loss between attempts, never open after `stopped` (shutdown's 1 s join bound, mod.rs:68, :392); end an attempt at once when its send put nothing out.
- F5 (minor): `open_device_locked` clears the stash on every tx_open (core.rs:1364-1366): reports stashed during attempt k are lost; record them first or state the exception (UR-28).
- F6 (minor): arm fixes s = a + 2 s before the opens (mod.rs:541-543, x3xx.rs:408); worst case 3 × (open + 100 ms) per TX stream, open duration unmeasured; in a Run the owner is blocked 3× longer: say so in the KL text.
